Government & Concord

Goetchius, The Language of the New Testament, §§30–31

Important devices which are found more frequently in Greek than in English are those called government and concord. In government various inflectional forms are used to indicate the function of a word in the grammatical structure in which it occurs…

In the grammatical device called concord certain words are required to have forms (indicated by inflections) which correspond in specified ways with the forms of other words to which they are grammatically related.
