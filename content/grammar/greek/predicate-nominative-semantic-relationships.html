Predicate Nominative Semantic Relationships

Wallace, Greek Grammar Beyond the Basics, pp. 41-42

There are two types of semantic relationships within the Subject-Predicate Nominative contstruction:

  1. Subset proposition: the PN describes a class to which the S belongs (e.g., "Jesus was a man"). Here, the verb "is" does not mean equals.
  2. Convertable proposition: The PN is an exact/identical exchange with the S (e.g., "Jesus is the Son of God"). Here, the verb "is" does mean equals.

The relationship can be determined by inverting the sentence: e.g., "The Word was flesh" is not the same as "Flesh is the Word", therefore this is a subset proposition.
