Semitic Substitution for Predicate Nominative

Wallace, Greek Grammar Beyond the Basics, p. 47

εἰς + accusative is occasionally found replacing the PN in the NT. This is usually due to Semitic influence (Hebrew ל). This idiom is frequently found in OT quotations.

This construction occurs with (1) γίνομαι; (2) εἰμί (typically in the future tense); and (3) λογίζομαι (infrequently).
